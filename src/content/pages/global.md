## Page titles and meta descriptions

<!-- note: under 60 characters for titles, under 155 for descriptions. Written for a person
reading a search result, not for a keyword. -->

**Home**
Title: Budget for what your older car costs to keep
Description: We buy your car at an agreed value and lease it back, so you can set aside money for what it costs to keep, part of it before tax.

**How it works**
Title: How sale and leaseback works
Description: Four steps, from agreeing a value to getting your running costs reimbursed, and a
diagram of where the money goes.

**Is my car eligible?**
Title: Is your car eligible?
Description: Six questions and about a minute. What matters is what the car costs to run
relative to what it's worth.

**Calculator**
Title: What a novated lease looks like on your numbers
Description: An indicative comparison between keeping your car as you are and packaging it.
Shows the residual, and shows a negative result when there is one.

**What it costs**
Title: What it costs
Description: An administration fee each pay cycle, and a lease rate on the agreed value of your
car. Both set out in your quote before you commit.

**For employers**
Title: For employers: what you're being asked to sign
Description: What a novation deed obliges you to do, what payroll sets up, and what happens
when an employee leaves.

**FAQ**
Title: Questions people ask
Description: Ownership, insurance, reimbursement, changing jobs and ending early.

**About**
Title: Why this exists
Description: Novated leasing is built around new cars, but packaging the running costs can work well on older ones.

**Contact**
Title: Tell us what you drive
Description: Send us the car and what it costs to run, and we'll come back with a quote.
