## H2 — We don't take a commission on the funding

We don't take a commission on the funding of your car. Where a financier is involved we take the lowest lease payment we can get you, and we're paid by our administration fee. [PRI-030]

We own the car and we lease it to you. [OWN-010, OWN-040]

## H2 — We don't sell add-ons

We don't sell paint protection, extended warranties or insurance commissions. [PRI-040] Those products
are where a lot of this industry makes its margin, and packaging them before tax makes a weak
product look cheap per pay cycle while you pay for it over the whole term.

If we ever think an add-on is worth having, we'll tell you what it costs and why, and you can
buy it wherever you like.

## H2 — You get our pricing in full when we quote

Everything you'd pay is set out in the quote, before you commit to anything. [PRI-060] The administration fee is listed as its own line. [PRI-070]

We don't publish the rate here because it moves with our funding costs, and a number on a
marketing page goes stale between the day it's written and the day you read it.

## H2 — We set our own criteria

We look at cars the mainstream providers turn away, and a car that falls outside one of our
rules can still be worth a conversation. [ELG-010]

## H2 — Keeping a car you already have

The CO2-e emissions from manufacturing your car occurred years ago. [ENV-010] We make it easier
for you to keep your car and provide for its maintenance, so you don't have to buy a new one.
[ENV-020] Keeping a car that already exists avoids all of the CO2-e emitted in manufacturing a new
one. [ENV-030]
