## H1

Questions people ask

---

**Who owns the car?**

We do, for the term of the lease. [OWN-020] You keep possession and use of it. There's a residual amount to pay at the end, and you can buy the car
back by paying it. [EOT-010]



**What about insurance?**

You insure the car and note our interest on the policy, on an agreed value basis. [SVC-030] For an
unusual or older car that's worth having in its own right, because an agreed value policy pays out the value you and the insurer agreed, where a market value policy pays what a valuation guide says.

**Can I keep using my mechanic?**

Yes. Send us the invoice and we reimburse you.

**How does getting money back work?**

You pay for something, send us the tax invoice, and we reimburse you out of the balance that's
built up from your deductions. What you can claim back is limited to what's in there. [SVC-010]

If you know a large bill is coming, you can build the balance ahead of it. If something
unexpected happens before the balance has built up, talk to us and we'll work out how to cover
it.

**What happens to money I don't spend?**

It stays in your balance and rolls forward. If the arrangement ends, whatever's left is
refunded through your employer's payroll and taxed on the way out, because it was never taxed
on the way in. [SVC-020]

**Do I save the GST on the car?**

No. You're selling the car to us privately, and a
private seller isn't registered for GST, so there's no credit to claim. [GST-020] The GST saving
is on the running costs you package. [SAV-020]

**What if I change jobs?**

The novation with your old employer ends and we set one up with your new one. If your new
employer won't agree, the lease comes back to you and we'll work out the options with you.
[EMP-010]

**What if my pay drops, or I go on unpaid leave?**

Tell us before it happens if you can. The deductions have to come from somewhere, and there are
a few ways to handle it depending on how long it lasts.

**Can I end the lease early?**

Yes, and it costs money. We'll set out the cost before you sign anything. [EOT-020]

**What if the car is written off?**

Your insurer pays out on the agreed value. Whether that covers what's owed depends on the
timing, and it's the reason the agreed value on your policy matters.

**Is this financial advice?**

No. This is general information only. It doesn't take account of your objectives, financial
situation or needs, and it isn't tax, credit or financial advice. Get your own advice before
you decide. [TAX-030]
