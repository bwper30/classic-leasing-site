## Intro

One of your employees wants to package a car they already own. That needs your agreement, and
this page sets out what you'd be agreeing to.

## H2 — The novation deed

The deed is between three parties: you, your employee, and us. It transfers the employee's
obligations under the lease to you for as long as they're employed and the arrangement is
running.

In practice that means you deduct an agreed amount from their pay and pass it to us. You don't
take on the vehicle, you don't take on residual risk, and your obligation ends when their
employment does. [EMP-010]

## H2 — Payroll

Set up two deduction lines and leave them alone.

A pre-tax deduction covering the agreed portion of the lease rental and the running-cost budget. A post-tax
deduction, if an employee contribution is being used to reduce the fringe benefits taxable
value to nil. [TAX-010]

Both amounts come from us in writing when the lease starts. They change only if the employee
asks for a change, and we'll confirm any change in writing before it takes effect.

## H2 — FBT and reporting

The car is a fringe benefit and it's your FBT liability, in the same way it would be with any
other novated lease. Where an employee contribution is used, the taxable value is reduced.
[TAX-010]

We provide the figures you need for your FBT return and for reportable fringe benefits on the
payment summary, on the same schedule you'd expect from any provider.

## H2 — Cost to you

Nothing. We don't charge employers a fee, and there's no minimum number of employees or panel arrangement to enter. Our fee is charged to the employee and it's disclosed in their quote. [PRI-070]

## H2 — If the employee leaves

The novation ends. Your deductions stop and your obligations stop with them.

Anything left in the employee's packaging balance is refunded through your payroll and taxed on
the way out, because it was never taxed on the way in. [SVC-020] We'll tell you the amount and the
treatment.

The lease itself doesn't end. It goes back to the employee, and they can novate it to a new
employer or deal with it directly with us. That's a matter between them and us.

## H2 — Your exposure

You aren't lending anything, guaranteeing anything, or taking on an asset. You're operating a
payroll deduction and signing a deed that ends when the employment does.

We're a new business. If that's a concern, the questions worth asking us are who
owns the vehicle, what happens to the employee's balance if we fail, and what the deed obliges
you to do if we do. We'll answer all three in writing.
