## H1

Put aside a realistic amount for what your car costs to keep

## Sub-heading

Older cars cost more to maintain, and the bills don't arrive evenly. A novated lease lets you
set money aside for them every pay cycle, so you're building a balance ahead of the work rather
than meeting it out of whatever is left at the end of the month.

## Body

We buy your car at an agreed value and lease it back to you. [OWN-010] A novation agreement then
passes the responsibility for the lease payments to your employer, who deducts the amount from
your pay. [EMP-010] You keep driving the same car and you service it where you always have.

Part of what's deducted covers the lease rental. The rest builds a fund you draw on for running
costs — fuel, servicing, tyres, registration and insurance. [SAV-010]

Once the balance has built up it should cover most of the work your car needs. If something
unexpected lands before then, we can look at varying your deductions to help you work through
it. [SVC-040]

Setting the money aside this way has three advantages over doing it yourself.

**A portion of the deductions comes out of your pay before tax.** [SAV-010, SAV-060] How much is
pre-tax depends on your circumstances, and we set it out in your quote.

**Running costs are generally net of GST**, because the input tax credits are claimable by your
employer. [SAV-020]

**Depreciation on an older car is a smaller number.** Depreciation is usually the largest cost
of running a new car, and a car that has already done most of its depreciating loses far less
in dollar terms over the same period. [SAV-070]

## H2 — Before you go further

<!-- note: not a benefits grid. The first is a constraint stated as a feature, honestly. -->

### The fund can only be spent on the car

It pays the lease rental and the running costs and nothing else. You can't dip into it for an
overseas holiday or a new kitchen. [SVC-050]

As many people put off maintenance until it becomes urgent and then have to pay using the
credit card, that restriction can be extremely beneficial.

### The running costs are what gets packaged

Being able to salary package running costs is what can make a novated lease worth looking at. On an older car those costs are the largest part of what you spend, and they're the
part the arrangement is built around. [SAV-050]

### Your employer needs to agree

They sign a novation agreement and run the deduction through payroll. [EMP-010] Some employers
have an exclusive novated leasing provider or a panel of acceptable providers. If yours
doesn't, we're happy to talk to them.
