## H1

What it costs

## Intro

An administration fee every pay cycle, and where we fund the car ourselves, a return on the
money we have tied up in it. [PRI-020]

## H2 — The administration fee

We charge an administration fee every pay cycle. The amount is set out in your quote and in
your contract. [PRI-070]

It's quoted rather than published because it isn't the same for everyone. It's built from a
base amount plus a component that varies with how much you claim through the year, so someone
running a car with heavy maintenance costs more to administer than someone running a car that
mostly needs fuel and a service. Publishing a single figure would mean either overcharging the
first person or underquoting the second.

## H2 — The return on the car

Your lease rental is based on the agreed value of the car and a lease rate that reflects the
cost of the funds used to buy it. [PRI-050]

The rate moves with our funding costs, so it's set out in your quote rather than here. [PRI-060]

## H2 — What we don't charge for

We don't take a commission on the funding of your car [PRI-030], and we don't sell paint protection, extended warranties or insurance commissions. [PRI-040]

## H2 — Whether it's worth it

For most people, the tax savings will exceed our charges. [PRI-020] Whether that's true for you
depends on your marginal rate, what the car costs to run, and what it's worth. The calculator works it out on your numbers, and it shows a negative result if that's what they produce.
