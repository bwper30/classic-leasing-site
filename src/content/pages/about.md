## H1

Why this exists

## Body

Novated leasing in Australia is built around new cars, and most providers set a minimum vehicle value that an older car won't meet.

The arrangement itself works whatever the car is worth. What a novated lease packages is the running costs, and running costs on a well-kept older car are often higher than on a new one, so the benefit can be larger on an older car than on a new one.

## H2 — How we're set up

We judge a car mainly on what it costs to run relative to what it's worth. [ELG-010]

We don't take a commission on the funding of your car, and we're paid by our administration fee. [PRI-030]

## H2 — What we won't do

We won't sell you paint protection, an extended warranty or an insurance policy we earn a
commission on. [PRI-040] Those products are a material part of how this industry makes money and
most of them don't pay out for the customer.

We won't publish a savings figure as though it's a promise. What you save depends on your
marginal rate, what your car costs to run and what it's worth, and for some people the answer
is that this isn't worth doing.

We own the car and we lease it to you, so we don't describe this as finance. [OWN-040]

## H2 — Keeping cars on the road

The CO2-e emissions from manufacturing your car occurred years
ago [ENV-010], and keeping a car that already exists avoids all of the CO2-e emitted in
manufacturing a new one. [ENV-030]

We make it easier for you to keep your car and provide for its maintenance, so you don't have to buy a new one. [ENV-020]
